About the content

Sources, schedule, exam flags, and what is missing

Where the content comes from

The app is built from a study guide (sta520-study-app-guide_1.md) synthesized from the course’s lecture slides (PDF/PPTX), expanded with textbook-level background explanations. The background prose is written independently from general molecular biology, genetics and biostatistics knowledge. It is not copied from the assigned textbook (Principles of Genetics and Molecular Epidemiology, Gomez-Verjan & Rivero-Segura, eds., Springer 2022).

The quizzes, drills, R exercises, figures, written model answers and the illustrative MR numbers in Lecture 6 were added for this app.

How to read the exam flags

Badge Meaning
🎯 Instructor-flagged EXAM The slide carried the literal text “EXAM” from the instructor. Lecture 7 only. The course’s reading guide confirms: “The slides with information that we will be testing on the exam are marked with a small text ‘EXAM.’”
📌 Likely exam-relevant (AI-inferred) An inference from what the slides present as a definition, formula, worked numerical example or something repeated across lectures. Not confirmed by the instructor.
(no badge) Background or context. Still course content.

WarningFile-to-session correction

“Lecture 3_genetics.pdf” was actually presented at the start of the Lecture 4 session, as a recap (“the same lecture notes as posted for lecture 3”). In this app, Lecture 3 merges “Lecture 3_recap and mutations.pdf” and “Lecture 3_genetics.pdf” by content. The genetics fundamentals (Mendel, LD, SNP arrays, HWE; Sections 3.6–3.9) were therefore taught in the Lecture 4 session, just before “Lecture 4 from SNP to GWAS.pdf”. This matters if an exam question refers to material “from lecture 4”.

What is missing

These exist in the course but were not available when the study guide was written, so nothing here is built from them:

  • “Lecture 7 - questions.pdf”: the instructor’s example exercises and possible exam questions. The most valuable missing file. Do it from Canvas.
  • The full siNET article (Arvidsson et al., Modern Pathology 2018): more methodological detail for Lecture 7.
  • Computer exercise1.docx and Computer exercise 2.docx: the PLINK exercise instructions. Lectures 5–6 cover only the conceptual lecture content, not the hands-on steps.
  • Raw exercise data: Lecture4_ex1.csv, jointGwasMc_LDL.txt, simulated_LDL.pheno, all_hg38_subset.*, CAD_Summary_filtered.txt.
  • “STA520 Literature list.pdf” and “STA520 Syllabus.pdf” returned no extractable text (probably scanned), so only “STA520 Course Syllabus.docx” was used for the course context.
  • The assigned textbook was deliberately not reproduced (copyright).
  • Two uploads of “Lecture 3_recap and mutations.pdf” existed; only one was read. No personal comments or annotations were found in any slide PDF.
  • Sessions from week 4 day 2 onward (“Study design, bias and interpretation”, “Integrating molecular data”, the seminar) have no slides in the guide yet.

If any of these become available, they can be added as new sections or questions.
